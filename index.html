Every claim.

Built by Shreekant Sharma, a supply-chain and logistics professional with close to two decades of hands-on experience running multi-city operations, vendor networks and field teams. As founder of Shield Infra Solutions Pvt. Ltd., he saw first-hand how manual expense claims, delayed reimbursements and untracked advances quietly drain both employee trust and company margins. Expense Easy was built to fix that — one platform that takes a claim from submission to settlement, with the same discipline expected on a factory floor: clear approval limits, an audit trail for every rupee, and reports finance teams can act on immediately.
